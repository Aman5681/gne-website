CREDIBILITY & INNOVATION

Why Industry Leaders Choose Lineage Power

From utility developers and tier-1 EPC contractors to telecom tower operators, we deliver uncompromised bankability, automated Indian manufacturing, and certified lifecycle reliability.

01

5 GWh Automated Bengaluru Gigafactory

Purpose-built advanced manufacturing complex with robotic cell-to-pack assembly lines, precision laser welding, and automated automated optical inspection (AOI) guaranteeing microscopic cell tolerance.

  • Fully traceable cell serial numbers via MES
  • In-house CNC cabinet fabrication & 7-tank coating
  • Direct OEM pricing with no intermediary overhead
02

Active Liquid Cooling Technology

Industrial-grade multi-channel liquid coolant plates maintain cell temperatures within a tight ±2.5°C delta, doubling battery operational lifespan and preventing localized thermal degradation in 50°C Indian summers.

  • Uniform heat dissipation across 5,000+ cells
  • 30% lower auxiliary energy consumption than air cooling
  • Integrated flow sensors and automatic pressure relief
03

Tier-1 EPC Bankability & Public Utilities

Trusted partner to top-tier infrastructure conglomerates including L&T Construction for mega state grid projects (Bihar Kajra Solar Park) and major national telecom operators.

  • ₹1,994.20 Million single landmark order capability
  • Full compliance documentation for public tenders
  • Tested to UL9540A, IEC 62619, and NFPA 855 codes
04

Multi-Tier BMS & ISO 27001 Cloud SCADA

Three-tier safety architecture (Cell, Rack, Container) paired with Central NOC cloud telemetry monitoring voltage, internal resistance, and state-of-charge with millisecond response times.

  • AI anomaly detection prevents thermal runaway
  • Sub-second emergency breaker isolation
  • Encrypted telemetry certified to ISO 27001:2022
