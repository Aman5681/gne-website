Eight Pillars of Turnkey Lifecycle Engineering

Our senior power systems engineers execute an end-to-end disciplined lifecycle protocol to eliminate thermal degradation risks, guarantee round-trip efficiency (RTE), and maximize 15-year asset ROI.

01
GRID MODELING
● PRE-FEASIBILITY

Application Engineering & Power Systems Sizing

High-precision simulation of Point of Interconnection (POI) at 11 kV through 220 kV. Our power engineers evaluate feeder thermal constraints, solar/wind curtailment duration curves, and regional time-of-day (ToD) tariff arbitrage windows to calculate exact container counts, C-rate duty cycles, and auxiliary power consumption.

02
FACTORY QA & FAT
● 100% PRE-SHIP BURN-IN

String-Level Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)

Every 5 MWh containerized block undergoes 100% full-load electrical and thermal burn-in testing prior to transport. Senterra engineers execute full C-rate charge/discharge cycles in climate-controlled testing bays (-10°C to +55°C), balancing active liquid chiller flow rates to guarantee cell-to-cell delta-T within ±2.5°C alongside aerosol safety trip tests.

100% Full-Load Power Burn-In Thermal Chiller Flow Calibration Dielectric & Fault Injection Validation
